Research & transparency

Built to help you decide — not decide for you.

Career-test results are directional, not a psychological assessment. Salary figures are general, order-of-magnitude estimates — not a quote, and not specific to any one employer. Role information draws on BLS, O*NET, and common industry reporting; each career profile links its primary source, and you should verify current expectations against real internship postings.

thisISyourplace is built for the BYU Information Systems program, helping students explore real career paths and prepare for the interviews that lead to them. Career and interview content was drafted with AI assistance, then fact-checked against primary sources — the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics and O*NET — with each career profile linking its specific source. Answers you type in Interview Prep are saved locally in your browser only.
